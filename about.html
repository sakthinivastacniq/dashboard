Whoosh Group connection

Established property expertise. A greener direction.

Whoosh Singapore Pte Ltd was founded in 2008 by S. Thiruchelvam. Its published services include repair and redecoration, waterproofing, M&E maintenance, demolition and reinstatement, and related property works.

Canopia focuses that property-service perspective on landscape design, greenery upkeep and softscape improvement. The group relationship is brand positioning; Whoosh Canopia is not being presented as an independently incorporated subsidiary.
